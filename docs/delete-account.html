Request deletion of your Health Connect account

Health Connect is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME]. This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your information when you do.

You can ask us to delete your Health Connect account at any time, and you do not need to have the app installed to do so. There are two ways to make the request.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Health Connect and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account is closed immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Use this if you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in.

  1. Send an email to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] with the subject Account deletion request.
  2. Include the full name and the mobile number or email address registered to the account, so we can identify it.
  3. We will confirm receipt and may ask one question to verify the account is yours. We will never ask you for your password.
  4. We complete the deletion within [RESPONSE PERIOD] and email you to confirm.
We will never ask for your password. If anyone claiming to be from Health Connect asks for your password or card details in order to delete your account, it is not us.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted we remove:

  • Your name, email address and mobile number
  • Your date of birth, gender and residential address
  • Your national identity number
  • Your profile photograph
  • Identity documents you uploaded, including both sides of your ID
  • Professional certificates and registration details, if you are a healthcare provider
  • Saved location history attached to your account
  • Push notification tokens and device information

Once this is done your account cannot be recovered, and you will no longer be findable or contactable through Health Connect.

What is kept, and for how long

We cannot delete everything, and we would rather tell you plainly than promise otherwise. Some records must be retained by law and by the rules that govern healthcare providers.

Where we keep these records, we remove the details that identify you personally wherever we can do so without defeating the reason the record is kept. At the end of the period shown above, the remaining records are deleted.

Deleting your account does not cancel a consultation in progress

If you have a consultation booked or under way, please complete or cancel it before deleting your account.
