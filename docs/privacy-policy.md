## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information the Health Connect mobile application
collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you
have over it.

It applies to everyone who uses Health Connect, whether as a **patient** or as a
**healthcare provider** (doctor, nurse, physiotherapist, social worker or pharmacist).

Health Connect handles **health information**, which is sensitive. We treat it accordingly,
and Section 11 sets out the specific commitments that apply to it.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Information you give us when you register

**All users:** full name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, gender, residential
address, town or city, region, and your **national identity number**.

**Healthcare providers additionally:** HPCNA registration number and its expiry date,
governing council, specializations, years of experience, professional biography, and — for
pharmacists — registered trading name, company registration number, business email, pharmacy
council number and practice number.

We collect the national identity number and professional registration details to verify that
you are who you say you are and, for providers, that you hold a valid licence to practise.
We do not use them for any other purpose, and we never use them for advertising.

### 3.2 Documents and images you upload

Profile photograph; identity document (front and back); primary and annual qualification
certificates; HPCNA certificate; prescribing or dispensing certificate; images attached to
support requests; and any medical images you attach to a consultation.

### 3.3 Health information

The ailment category you select, the symptoms you describe in your own words, the
consultation mode you choose, records of consultations you have had, and prescriptions issued
to you.

### 3.4 Location information

We collect your **precise location** when you request a consultation, and the address it
resolves to (street, suburb, town, region). This is essential to matching you with a nearby
provider and, for home visits, to allow the provider to reach you.

Providers additionally share their live location with the patient while travelling to a
confirmed visit.

### 3.5 Payment information

**We do not collect or store your card details.** When you pay for a consultation package,
you are taken to a payment page hosted by our payment provider, DPO Pay, and your card
details are entered there. They are never sent to us and never stored on our systems.

We do record that a payment was made: the amount, a transaction reference, and which package
it relates to.

### 3.6 Consultation audio and video

For video consultations, live audio and video are carried between you and your provider by
our video provider, Stream.io. **[CONFIRM: state here whether calls are recorded. If they are
not recorded or stored anywhere, say so explicitly — it is a materially better position and
should be stated plainly. If they are recorded, state why, who can access recordings, and how
long they are kept.]**

### 3.7 Device and usage information

A push notification token, your device model and operating system version, your consultation
and request history, and whether you have read a notification.

We do **not** collect crash logs, analytics, advertising identifiers, your contacts, your
calendar, or your web browsing history. Health Connect contains no advertising and no
third-party analytics.

## 5. Who we share it with

### 5.1 Your healthcare provider

When you request a consultation, the provider who accepts it receives your name, mobile
number, location and address, your selected ailment category and the symptoms you have
described. This is the purpose of the service.

Where a prescription is issued, the dispensing pharmacist receives the prescription and the
information needed to dispense it.

### 5.2 Our verification staff

Identity documents and professional certificates are reviewed by authorised staff for the
sole purpose of verifying identity and licence validity.

### 5.3 Service providers

| Who | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Stream.io** | Live consultation audio and video, participant identifiers | Carries video consultations |
| **DPO Pay** | Payment amount, transaction reference, and the card details you enter on their own page | Processes payments |

| **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** | Push token, notification content | Delivers notifications to Android devices |

These providers act on our instructions, except DPO Pay, which is independently responsible
for the payment data you give it directly.

### 5.4 Legal and regulatory disclosure

We may disclose information where we are required to by law, by a court, or by a regulator
such as the Health Professions Councils of Namibia, or where disclosure is necessary to
protect someone's life or safety.

## 9. How we protect your information

- All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, and the
  app is configured to refuse unencrypted connections entirely
- Credentials and session tokens are held in the device's secure storage
- Sessions expire automatically, so a session cannot remain valid indefinitely
- Screenshot and screen-recording protection is enabled on sensitive screens
- Passwords are stored only in hashed form and are never readable by us or by our staff
- Card details never reach our systems

No system is completely secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect your rights, we
will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.
