2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information the Health Connect mobile application collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over it. It applies to everyone who uses Health Connect, whether as a patient or as a healthcare provider (doctor, nurse, physiotherapist, social worker or pharmacist).

Health Connect handles health information, which is sensitive. Section 11 sets out the specific commitments that apply to it.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you give us when you register

All users: full name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, gender, residential address, town or city, region, and your national identity number.

Healthcare providers additionally: HPCNA registration number and expiry date, governing council, specializations, years of experience, professional biography, and — for pharmacists — registered trading name, company registration number, business email, pharmacy council number and practice number.

We collect the national identity number and professional registration details to verify who you are and, for providers, that you hold a valid licence to practise. We do not use them for any other purpose, and never for advertising.

3.2 Documents and images you upload

Profile photograph; identity document (front and back); primary and annual qualification certificates; HPCNA certificate; prescribing or dispensing certificate; images attached to support requests; and any medical images you attach to a consultation.

3.3 Health information

The ailment category you select, the symptoms you describe in your own words, the consultation mode you choose, records of consultations you have had, and prescriptions issued to you.

3.4 Location information

We collect your precise location when you request a consultation, and the address it resolves to. This is essential to matching you with a nearby provider and, for home visits, allowing them to reach you. Providers additionally share their live location with the patient while travelling to a confirmed visit.

3.5 Payment information

We do not collect or store your card details. When you pay for a consultation package you are taken to a payment page hosted by our payment provider, DPO Pay, and your card details are entered there. They are never sent to us and never stored on our systems.

We do record that a payment was made: the amount, a transaction reference, and which package it relates to.

3.6 Consultation audio and video

For video consultations, live audio and video are carried between you and your provider by our video provider, Stream.io. [CONFIRM: state whether calls are recorded. If they are not recorded or stored anywhere, say so explicitly. If they are, state why, who can access them, and for how long.]

3.7 Device and usage information

A push notification token, your device model and operating system version, your consultation and request history, and whether you have read a notification.

We do not collect crash logs, analytics, advertising identifiers, your contacts, your calendar, or your web browsing history. Health Connect contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

5. Who we share it with

5.1 Your healthcare provider

The provider who accepts your consultation receives your name, mobile number, location and address, your selected ailment category and the symptoms you have described. This is the purpose of the service. Where a prescription is issued, the dispensing pharmacist receives the prescription and the information needed to dispense it.

5.2 Our verification staff

Identity documents and professional certificates are reviewed by authorised staff solely to verify identity and licence validity.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your Health Connect account in two ways:

In the app: open Profile, then Delete account, and confirm. [CONFIRM final navigation path.]

On the web, without installing the app: visit [DELETION URL] and follow the steps there.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address and mobile number
  • Your date of birth, gender and residential address
  • Your national identity number
  • Your profile photograph and uploaded identity documents
  • Professional certificates and registration details, if you are a provider
  • Saved location history
  • Push notification tokens and device information

Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered and you will no longer be findable or contactable through Health Connect.

What is kept, and why

We cannot delete everything, and we would rather tell you plainly than promise otherwise.

Transaction records are retained for [RETENTION PERIOD] to meet financial and tax obligations.

Where records are retained, we remove the details that identify you personally wherever we can do so without defeating the purpose the records are kept for. At the end of the applicable period, the remaining records are deleted.

9. How we protect your information

  • All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, and the app refuses unencrypted connections entirely
  • Credentials and session tokens are held in the device's secure storage
  • Sessions expire automatically and cannot remain valid indefinitely
  • Screenshot and screen-recording protection is enabled on sensitive screens
  • Passwords are stored only in hashed form and are never readable by us or our staff
  • Card details never reach our systems

No system is completely secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.
